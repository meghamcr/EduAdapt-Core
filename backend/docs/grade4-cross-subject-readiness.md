### Educational forms and remaining review considerations

- **Passages:** Chandan's introduction, the multi-page Van Mahotsav dialogue and
  narrative, bridge example, community meal and cultural/community examples.
  Retain questions with their relevant passages and preserve continuation order.
- **Explanatory notes sources:** community interdependence, public-place functions,
  occupations/tools, cooperation and environmental-care explanations. These supply exact development Notes; learner delivery still requires curriculum approval and explicit server authorization.
- **Examples:** supplied table entries, example interview questions, cooperation
  stories and real-world community practices. Do not reduce these to bare labels.
- **Activities:** locality observation, poster making, interviews, group plantation/
  cleanliness/craft activities, action planning, reflection tables and riddle writing.
- **Investigations:** open observation, interviews and local skill/craft inquiry.
  These are not necessarily formal experimental investigations; preserve their
  actual response requirements instead of forcing a mathematics-style hierarchy.
- **Teacher guidance:** unit-level facilitation, visits, skilled-person interaction,
  observation materials and the poster-material note. Distinguish this guidance
  from student-facing prose and from the Chapter 2 unit overview.
- **Numerical/contextual givens:** dates, group sizes, duration and numbered subparts
  must remain source-backed. Personal observations, blank tables and riddle answers
  must not be filled by ingestion.
- **Visual dependencies:** the village scene, pictures of workers/tools, community
  illustrations and riddle pictures. Preserve textual prompts and reliable captions;
  do not infer identities, counts or label-to-picture associations.
