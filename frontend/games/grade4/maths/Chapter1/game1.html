📖 WHAT IS WHAT: DIAGRAM LESSONS

ACUTE ANGLE (<90°)

An angle smaller than a Right Angle (<90°). Found in open scissors and yoga poses.

RIGHT ANGLE (=90°)

An exact square corner equals 90°. Made using a double-folded paper checker.

OBTUSE ANGLE (>90°)

An angle larger than a Right Angle (>90°). Found in clock hands at 4:00.

CIRCLE CREASES & SHAPE RIGIDITY

🔴 Diameter (D): Crease line passing through Center A connecting both borders.

🟢 Radius (R): Distance from Center A to border. $Radius = \frac{1}{2} \times Diameter$.

🔺 Triangle Rigidity: Triangles do NOT deform when pushed; square straw frames bend into rhombuses!
